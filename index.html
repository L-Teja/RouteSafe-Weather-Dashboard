Safety and data quality

Understand what the dashboard can and cannot verify

☀️

Heat safety

Drink water, seek shade and reduce strenuous activity during peak heat.

🌧️

Heavy rainfall

Do not enter flooded roads. Rain forecasts do not confirm road flooding.

🚧

Road reports

Community reports are unverified until checked by a trusted source.

📡

Data limitations

Missing live data must not be interpreted as proof that conditions are safe.

For emergencies in India, call 112 where available. Follow official meteorological and local disaster-management advisories.
